What happens?
You get a desk of your own, which you sign in to with your email address and password. There you pick a story, and keikaku translates the whole of it with your key. The result is published on Fortleben under your name. You can have one translation going at a time, and the site takes only a few at once. If it's full, the desk says so; try again a little later.
Your key?
Make a key for this and nothing else. Put on it only what you are willing to spend; start small, you can add more. Turn off automatic top-up so a run can never spend more than you put on it. A short story costs about as much as a coffee. If the key runs out part-way, the translation stops where it is. Top it up and resume it from the desk; nothing is paid for twice.
What we do with it?
We hold it encrypted on our server and use it only for your translations. It is never shown or logged, and you see it again only as its last four characters. Press Forget this key on your desk and it is gone.
How the edition names you.
For signing in, and for a reset link if you forget your password. Nothing else is sent to it.
At least 8 characters.
From the Meta Model API's dashboard.
