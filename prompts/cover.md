# Cover

Not an agent: an image-prompt template for `muse-image`. Decorative, cut first.

A cover for the edition, built from the house design (riso texture, the house blue, the work's own accent colour) and one image drawn from the reading: the thing the text turns on, rendered plainly. No text on the image; the title is set in HTML. No faces of real people. No pastiche of ukiyo-e or of any living artist's style.

## Inputs

The reading's imagery, the accent colour, the house palette.

## Output

One image prompt in the template's shape; the image is generated by code.
