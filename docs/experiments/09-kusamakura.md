# 9. A third text: 『草枕』, chapter II

**Question.** Does the house hold up on a third, quite different text? Sōseki's chapter II (1906) has two voices: the painter-narrator's lofty, allusive reflections (Takasago, Millais's *Ophelia*, the feather robe of the Noh play), and the plain, old-fashioned speech of a mountain tea-house's old woman. It has 109 paragraphs in 5,600 characters, mostly short lines of dialogue, and a single coined word the whole novel turns on: 非人情.

**Set-up.** The house on its defaults: the plain drafting prompt, Spark readers, a Wonder desk, a Spark final read. The chapter is taken from Aozora Bunko's file of the whole novel (`fixtures/works.json`, `chapter: 二`). It's audited blind beside the one-shot and Kazutomo Takahashi's *Unhuman Tour* (1927), a published human translation in dated English.

## How the run went

- **The cost was $1.07, against an estimate of $1.72.**
- **The planner made the chapter one part,** "a single continuous tea-house episode".
- **The readers raised 29 notes;** the desk took 26 and sent 3 up as forks. All three are real questions for an editor:
  - the two independent first reads named different cruxes for the chapter;
  - whether 青馬 is a "blue horse" or a period word for a horse's coat;
  - how to render 非人情.
- **The final read made 4 fixes.** No call failed and none fell back to Spark.

## The blind audit

| | Fidelity (critical / serious / minor) | Translationese (serious / minor) | Rank |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The house** | 0 / 3 / 10 | 2 / 8 | 1 |
| One-shot | 0 / 5 / 12 | 3 / 12 | 2 |
| Takahashi (1927) | 1 / 5 / 11 | 2 / 10 | 3 |

- **The house ranked first,** as "the most faithful and complete… It carries the narrator's lofty, allusive passages (Takasago, Ophelia, the feather robe, the floating world) with real dignity."
- **Takahashi is an abridgement.** About fifteen paragraphs become one sentence ("We kept on talking about the quietness of the place, the uguisu, and so on."), the verse is left untranslated, and some things are added. Yet it gets several readings right that both machine versions miss: 近づき as "acquaintances", Millais rather than "Millet", and the Shioda as the village's rich family.
- **The one-shot is complete,** but it keeps Japanese clause order in exactly the loftiest passages. It also puts the old woman at her own wedding, where the wedding is the Nakoi daughter's.

**Where the house fell short:**
- **Its dialogue stiffens from mid-chapter.** "Is it not so, Oba-san." / "A worry it is."
- **Too many romanised words are left bare:** *Oba-san*, *Danna*, *shisshi*.
- **非人情 became "non-emotion",** which suggests emotionlessness. The auditor preferred Takahashi's "unhuman". The faithful reader had raised it, and the desk sent it to the editor as a fork rather than settle it.
- **The faithful reader was right about 青馬,** a black horse, but the desk kept "blue horse" and sent it up. The fork is where an editor would catch it.

## What it shows

- **Three texts, three first places.** The house came first on 「晩餐」, 「酒虫」 and now this chapter. Each time it was first on faithfulness and close on the English.
- **The same stiffness appears on a third text.** Uncontracted, careful dialogue and long sentences in Japanese order are the gap that remains. They sit in the draft, and the readers and the desk only patch them.
- **The forks work as designed.** The house's three hardest questions on this chapter went to the editor, written out, instead of being settled silently.
